TxLINE Recovery Activate

Recovery tool for an already finalized subscription. It does not re-run subscribe. It only fetches a fresh guest JWT, signs txSig::jwt with Phantom, and calls activation through your Worker.

Open this page in Phantom browser on mobile or in a desktop browser with Phantom installed. Your Worker must already support txline_guest_start and txline_activate, with activation forwarding leagues: [].
